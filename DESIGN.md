# Design System: The Captain's Watch

A dark, Capitano-inspired variant of the portfolio at aucs0n.github.io.

## Mood
Night watch on a frozen front line: calm, disciplined, protective. Security work is framed as "holding the line".

## Palette
- Surfaces: deep night slate `#0b1016` → `#223040`
- Primary (frost): `#9fd3ff`
- Tertiary (brass, for chips and emblems): `#c9a45c` / `#e8cf98`
- Text: `#e6edf3`, muted `#a9b6c3`

## Type
- Headings: Cinzel (engraved, military feel)
- Body: Plus Jakarta Sans

## Motifs
Shield icon as the emblem, snowy mountains under a starry sky, brass chips for tags.
